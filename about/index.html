AutoWallX is a vision to turn the most looked-at part of your life—your phone screen—into a source of constant inspiration and mindfulness.

Welcome to AutoWallX, the ultimate personalization hub for Android. We combine elite 4K artistry with intelligent features that respect your time and energy.

The AutoWallX Philosophy

Most people unlock their phones over 100 times a day. We believe every one of those interactions is an opportunity to be inspired. AutoWallX was built to move beyond static images and bring your background to life with purpose.

1. Live Life Countdown (Our Signature Feature)

Visualize your journey with our unique Progress Tracker. By entering your birth date and expected lifespan, the app generates a beautiful grid of dots on your wallpaper. Each dot represents a day of your life—reminding you that every single day is an asset to be used wisely.

2. Days Challenge Mode

Whether you're building a new habit (21-Day Challenge) or tracking a 90-day goal, AutoWallX keeps your progress front and center. Your wallpaper updates as you conquer each day, keeping your motivation levels at their peak.
