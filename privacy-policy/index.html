Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 27, 2026

AutoWallX ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is necessary to provide the core services of AutoWallX:

  • Account Information: If you sign in via Firebase, we store your email address to sync your favorite wallpapers across devices.
  • Interaction Data: We track which categories you interact with to personalize your experience (e.g., category-based shuffling).
  • Device Information: General info like Android version and device model for app performance monitoring and bug reporting.

2. Wallpapers and Data Usage Optimization

  • Thumbnail Usage: To save your mobile data and provide a fast experience, we load low-resolution versions for browsing.
  • High-Resolution Content: Full-quality images are only accessed when you explicitly view, download, or set a wallpaper.
  • No Personal Media Access: AutoWallX does NOT access, read, or upload your personal photos, videos, or files from your device.

3. Device Permissions

We request permissions only when required by a specific feature:

  • Media/Storage: To save high-quality wallpapers to your device's Gallery.
  • Notifications: To provide content updates and service status.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase: Used for secure Authentication and Crash Reporting.
  • Supabase & Cloudflare: Used to securely deliver wallpaper metadata at high speeds.
  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device-specific IDs for relevant ads.

5. Data Security & Retention

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. You can delete your account and associated favorites at any time by contacting us at autowallx@gmail.com. Requests are processed within 48 hours.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please reach out:

Email: autowallx@gmail.com
