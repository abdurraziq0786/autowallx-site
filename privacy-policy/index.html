Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 27, 2026

AutoWallX ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is necessary to provide the core services of AutoWallX:

  • Account Information: If you sign in via Firebase, we store your email address to sync your favorite wallpapers across devices.
  • Interaction Data: We track which categories you interact with to personalize your experience (e.g., category-based shuffling).
  • Device Information: General info like Android version and device model for app performance monitoring and bug reporting (via Firebase Analytics).

2. Wallpapers and Data Usage Optimization

  • Thumbnail Usage: To save your mobile data and provide a fast experience, we load low-resolution versions (thumbnail_url) for browsing.
  • High-Resolution Content: Full-quality images (image_url) are only accessed when you explicitly view, download, or set a wallpaper.
  • No Personal Media Access: AutoWallX does NOT access, read, or upload your personal photos, videos, or files from your device.

3. Device Permissions

We request the following permissions only when required by a specific feature:

  • Media/Storage: To save high-quality wallpapers to your device's Gallery.
  • Notifications: To provide "Smart Reminders" for new content and status updates for the "Auto Wallpaper" service.

You can manage or revoke these permissions at any time through your Android device settings.

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party providers to maintain the app's infrastructure and provide high-quality services:

  • Firebase (by Google): Used for secure Authentication, Analytics, and Crash Reporting.
  • Supabase & Cloudflare: Used to securely store and deliver wallpaper metadata and images at high speeds.
  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use device-specific IDs to provide relevant ads.

5. Auto Wallpaper Feature

Our "Auto Wallpaper" feature uses Android’s WorkManager API to schedule background changes. This feature operates entirely on-device based on the preferences you set within the app.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures (including encryption via Cloudflare and Firebase) to protect your account data. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

  • We keep your account data only as long as you have an active account.
  • Account Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated favorites/data at any time. Simply contact us at autowallx@gmail.com with your request, and we will process it within 48 hours.

8. Children’s Privacy

AutoWallX does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the application.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please reach out:

Email: autowallx@gmail.com
